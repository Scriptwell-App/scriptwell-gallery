# Golden Hour

When the light is best for photos today: golden hour, blue hour, sunrise and sunset.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app, Shortcuts action
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium, rectangular, inline
- **Permissions:** `network` to fetch data over HTTPS; `location` to read your current location

## Notes

Sunrise and sunset come from https://open-meteo.com (free, no key) for your location, rounded to about a kilometre. Golden hour is taken as the hour after sunrise and the hour before sunset, and blue hour as the 30 minutes outside them. That is a good rule of thumb, not an exact calculation.
