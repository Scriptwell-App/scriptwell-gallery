# Mood Journal

Log how you feel each day on a five-point scale with a note and tags. On-device AI pulls themes from your notes and writes a weekly reflection; a 12-week heatmap, weekday averages and tag counts show the patterns. Everything stays on the device.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app, Shortcuts action
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium, large, circular
- **Permissions:** none

## How it works

- Pick one of five moods, add an optional note and tags, and save. Entries are kept as one JSON file per month in the script's Files folder; export them all from the History tab.
- When Apple Intelligence is available, on-device AI extracts themes from your note and writes a short weekly reflection. Without it, your own tags are used and the reflection is built from simple statistics.
- The medium widget logs today's mood with one tap. The small widget shows today's mood, the large one the 12-week heatmap, and the Lock Screen circle shows this week's average.
- No permissions and no network: nothing leaves the device.
