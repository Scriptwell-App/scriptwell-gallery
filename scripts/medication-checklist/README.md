# Medication Checklist

A daily checklist for medicines and supplements, with reminders at the times you set.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium
- **Permissions:** `notifications` to schedule local notifications

## Notes

A personal checklist. It does not give medical advice; follow the instructions from your doctor or pharmacist.  Reminders are scheduled for the next three days every time you open the app or tick something off, so open it at least every few days.
