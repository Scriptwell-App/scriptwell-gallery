# Breathing

Box breathing guided by taps you can feel, one calm minute at a time.

- **Type:** Mini-app
- **Permissions:** none

## Notes

Box breathing: breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Tap Begin, rest your eyes, and follow the taps: one tap = breathe in, two taps = hold, three taps = breathe out. Each press guides one full breath (16 seconds).
