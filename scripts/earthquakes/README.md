# Earthquakes

Significant earthquakes around the world in the last 24 hours, from the USGS.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app, Shortcuts action
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium, large
- **Permissions:** `network` to fetch data over HTTPS

## Notes

Data: the U.S. Geological Survey's public feed (free, no key) of every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 and above in the past day. To show only stronger ones, put a magnitude such as "6" in the widget's Parameter field.
