# Air Quality

The air quality index where you are, with fine-dust levels and plain advice.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app, Shortcuts action
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium, circular, inline
- **Permissions:** `network` to fetch data over HTTPS; `location` to read your current location

## Notes

Data: https://open-meteo.com air quality API (free, no key), for your location rounded to about a kilometre. The index is the US AQI. General guidance only; follow local health advice.
