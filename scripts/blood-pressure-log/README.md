# Blood Pressure Log

Record readings, see your weekly average and share the log with your doctor.

- **Type:** Widget, Mini-app, Shortcuts action
- **Widget sizes:** small, medium
- **Permissions:** none

## Notes

A personal log. It does not diagnose anything and is not medical advice: talk to a doctor about what your readings mean for you.  From Shortcuts, pass a reading such as "124/82" or "124/82 68" (with pulse).
